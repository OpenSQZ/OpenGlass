## Relationship to This Repository

The ACL 2026 System Demonstration corresponds to the OpenGlass-Core track:

- ESP32-S3 wearable sensing.
- Nearby-host local MLLM/VLM inference.
- Modular speech interaction through ASR, VLM, streaming text, and TTS.
- Task-oriented evaluation for visual assistance scenarios.
- Safety-aware abstention and timestamped logging.

The paper describes OpenGlass as a local-first sensing-computing split system. The large model runs on a nearby host, not on the ESP32 glasses.
